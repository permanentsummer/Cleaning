Privacy Policy

SummerFlow · Last updated: October 2026

SummerFlow is operated by Permanent Summer Lda (Portugal) ("we"). This policy explains how personal data is handled when hosts use SummerFlow and when guests use a host's booking page.

Who is responsible for what

For the data a host enters or collects (properties, reservations, guest names, emails, phone numbers, requests), the host is the data controller and SummerFlow acts as a processor storing and processing it on the host's behalf. For host account data (email, login), SummerFlow is the controller.

Data we process

  • Hosts: email, business name, phone, property and reservation data, settings.
  • Guests (via a host's booking page): name, email, phone, dates, number of guests, notes.

Why

To provide the service: managing reservations, sending booking confirmations and notifications, and showing availability. We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising.

Isolation

Each host can only access their own data. Guests' details are visible only to the host they booked with.

Service providers

We use Supabase (database and authentication, EU/US infrastructure) and an email delivery provider to send booking emails. Fonts may be loaded from Google Fonts.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while the account is active. A host can delete their account and all its data at any time from Account → Delete my account. Guests can ask their host, or us, to delete their data.

Your rights (GDPR)

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your data, and may complain to the Portuguese authority (CNPD). Contact: chris@permanentsummer.co.
