This policy explains how personal data is handled when hosts use SummerFlow and when guests use a host's booking page. It applies the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Portuguese data-protection law.

3. Cookies and local storage

SummerFlow uses only strictly necessary browser storage: your login session and a few preferences (language, theme, remembered view). There are no advertising or analytics cookies. Because they are strictly necessary, no consent banner is required.

International transfers

Where data goes outside the EEA, we rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (where the provider is certified) or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, plus the providers' own security measures.

5. How long we keep data

  • While your account is active: your data stays until you delete it.
  • Account deletion (Account → Delete my account): your data is removed from our live systems immediately; copies in encrypted backups, if any, are overwritten within 30 days.
  • Guest requests and bookings: kept by the host for as long as the host decides, and in any case deleted with the account. Hosts should not keep guest data longer than needed and legally required.
  • Security and email logs: a limited period (typically up to 90 days).
  • Accounting records we are legally required to keep: as the law requires (currently up to 10 years in Portugal).

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and stored with access control per account (row-level security), passwords are hashed, administrative access is limited, and public functions are rate-limited. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data we will notify you and the authority as the law requires.

7. Your rights

You can request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability and objection, and withdraw consent at any time, by writing to christopher@permanentsummer.co. Guests: for data entered on a host's booking page, please contact the host first; the host controls it. If we receive such a request we will forward it to the host or help the host respond. You may also complain to the Portuguese data-protection authority, the CNPD (www.cnpd.pt), or the authority in your own country.

8. Children

SummerFlow is for professionals and is not directed at children under 16. Hosts must not collect data from minors except what the law requires for a stay.

9. Processor terms (data processing agreement for hosts)

These terms apply between each host (controller) and SummerFlow (processor) under Article 28 GDPR for guest and booking data:

  • Subject and duration: providing the SummerFlow service while the account is active. Data and people: as in section 2 (guests and the host's staff). Instructions: we process the data only on the host's documented instructions, which are the host's use of the service and these terms.
  • Confidentiality: people with access are bound by confidentiality.
  • Security: the measures in section 6.
  • Sub-processors: those listed in section 4. We will inform hosts of material changes (by email or in the app) and hosts may object or end the service.
  • Assistance: we help hosts answer data-subject requests and comply with security and breach-notification duties, and notify the host without undue delay after becoming aware of a breach affecting their data.
  • End of service: on deletion the data is erased as in section 5, unless the law requires keeping it. Hosts can export their data before.
  • Audit: we make available the information necessary to show compliance and allow reasonable audits, on written request and notice.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. For material changes we will notify hosts by email or in the app.
